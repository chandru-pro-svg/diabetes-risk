About this model

Scikit-learn LogisticRegression (L2 penalty, C = 1.0, lbfgs solver), saved with version 1.6.1. The probability is the sigmoid of the intercept plus the weighted sum of the scaled inputs, and a result of 50% or higher is classified as positive.

The pickle file doesn’t include its feature scaler. Inputs here are min-max scaled using the standard Pima Indians dataset ranges, which reproduced sensible predictions on known records. If you trained with different preprocessing, update the ranges in the script.
